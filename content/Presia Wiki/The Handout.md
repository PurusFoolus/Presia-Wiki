# Terror by Kite!

The village of Orlane Is dying. Once a small and thriving community. Orlane has become a maze of locked doors and frightened faces. Strangers are shunned, trade has withered. Rumors flourish, growing wilder with each retelling. Terrified peasants flee their homes, abandoning their farms with no explanation. Others simply disappear...

No one seems to know the cause of the decay— why are there no clues? Who skulks through the twisted shadows of the night? Who or what is behind the doom that has overtaken the village?

Brave Adventurers, your help is gravely needed in this dark place. only you may free these people from that which ails them, and discover the dark secret that lies beneath the town of Orlane!

**Your characters start at level 2 and begin with their standard Class-based equipment.**

# Welcome to the world of Presia

An ancient land that precedes modern history, Presia is one of three known continent that spread across this globe. Presia is roughly the size of real world Europe, and is considered the Elvish homeland. Due to recent events, the outside world has crept into Presia and have made parts of it their home.

## Language
The world of Presia is vast and full of various peoples, each speaking their own native tongue. Below is a list of each language and the people that speak it. In place of learning "Common", you learn **Merchant's Tongue** and one additional language representing your homeland.

**Merchant's Tongue**: A simplified version of Elvish that's been used as a basis for communication between trade families all over the continent. Most educated people can hold a conversation in this language (**This Language replaces "Common")

**Dwerian**: This tough and mechanical language arrived with the Human colonists that arrived upon Presia a short time ago, and is only spoken within their borders, and some pockets within *The Free North*

**True Elvish**: Also known as just "Elvish", this elegant language is spoken by the Elven natives that still inhabit Noarun, as well as the nation of Alalsasaria. It's the most commonly spoken language outside of Givilith. (**This language "replaces" Elvish**)

**Undercommon**: A prehistoric language that was spoken by a caste of elves banished deep underground, but with the rise of surface Drow, the languages now enjoys a new life as the official tongue of the Givilian people. 

**Northern**: Very little is known about the Humans that dwell on Whitneseria, and even less so is known about their language.

**Barbarian's Tongue**: The language of the Hordish invaders, a brutish language that's closer to rambles and slurs than civilized speech. (**This language replaces Orc and Goblin**)

# History of...

## ...Dwerelith

The sole colony of its mother nation, *Dwere*. Originally belonging to the Elves of Alalsasaria, the island of Noarun has been occupied by Dwerian forces for the past half century. Humans made first contact with the Elves in 1931 PU (Post Union), and crushed the Elven military in the *First Contact* war. Dwerelith was officially signed into existence in 1933 PU as part of a peace treaty between Dwere and Alalsasaria. 

The capital city of Dwerelith is AussenStadt, which lies on the eastern coast of the island. The nation is governed by the *Dwerelith Commission*, under direct supervision of the Dwerian Parliment. 

Humans have low natural affinity for magic, and through many decades of trial and error, the art of *Artifice* has been born. Artificers form the upper caste of Human society. Thanks to them, Humanity's military might has advanced massively, with the invention of *Firearms*, *primitive land and air vehicles*, and ***The Warforged***

### Species Composition

**Humans**: Due to great colonization efforts Humans are now the dominant species in Dwerelith. Most cities are settled by and governed by humans brought from abroad, with a blooming population of Humans born in Noarun.

**Elves**: Many of the indigenous natives that used to live here have clung to their homes, though many have been forcefully deported into the Alalsasarian mainland. Many of the natives retain some connection to the Noan Witch Covens long after the witches were wiped out.

**Dragonborn**: Originally created as super-soldiers to serve in the *Unification War*, Dragonborn are now common folk that live amongst the humans, although much fewer in number. 

**Warforged**: Created by a great Artificer of renown, Warforged fueled the First Contact war and act as the vanguard of Humanity's struggle against *The Horde*. **Halflings**: Nobody knows where the halflings came from, but they were here when the first human tribes emerged on Patriam. Halflings are a reserved race, and tend to distance themselves from human politics as a rule. When the first colonial ships sailed to Presia, only a handful Halflings came alongside them.

### Religion

The humans of Dwere worship **The Six**, believed to be the ones that brought Humans safely from their original home beyond the stars to their new home in *Patriam*. The gods are as follows:

**Vita**: The goddess of Life, Healing, and the elements. Vita is at the head of the pantheon, and is represented as a winged woman.

**Mors**: The god of death and Vita's twin brother, Mors is a shady god that is almost never seen gracing the mortal realm. He's represented as a hooded figure wearing a deer skull.

**Amare**: Once a whole goddess, now she's been split into the twin goddesses Castidia and Miserta. Castidia rules over the domain of carnal desires, illusion, and deception while Miserta's domain encompasses platonic love, Compassion, and beauty. The goddesses are represented as beautiful women each wearing one half of a mask.

**Bellum**: A fiery deity that represents Humanity's lust for war and their desperation to cling onto their honor, Bellum overlooks the domains of War and Heroism. He's depicted as a long horned man wielding a greatsword.

**Vis**: Her graceful yet firm demeanor fits the goddess of Nature. Vis represents mother nature's kind side, and also her harsh and destructive side. She is usually represented as a fairy rooted into the ground by tree bark.

**Prudentia**: The youngest of the pantheon, he represents humanity's newfound ideals of Order, knowledge, and humanity's thirst for advancement. The young god is usually represented as a blindfolded man whose glowing eyes shine through to show the way.


## ...Orlane
Nestled deep within the Hauer forest is the Village of Orlane. A quaint little town that was constructed atop the remains of an Elven Witch Coven. This land was granted to general **Zakarias Ormond** whose contributions were tantamount to the Dwerian expansion efforts in the region. Zakarias constructed a small settlement to support him and his subordinates, and slowly it grew into a fully fledged village.

Amongst the newly arrived humans thrives a small population of Elven refugees who had previously lived in the nearby City of Irnthyr (Modern Day Weißer Fluss). Under the new situation that the village finds itself in, fingers are pointed towards the Elves as the cause for such catastrophe.

A large temple constructed to honor the goddess Vis was constructed as a gift by the Duke of Hainaut as a gift to the people of Orlane, alongside Misha Devi, who is to serve as the Priestess.

Very recently, the residents of Orlane have started acting strangely and many have gone missing. Many rumors circulate regarding the strange happenings in Orlane, such that nefarious actors have poisoned the well water with a powerful drug or that a powerful Witch has found refuge in the Hauer forest and is the cause of all of the strangeness.

# A Brief Overview of...


## ...Alalsasaria
The ancient empire of Alalsasaria used to span over the entire continent at one point in time, but now they are a shadow of their former selves. Following the assassination of Emperor Imhariel, his second in command (Magus Fyrita) has now taken the throne. 

Due to their higher affinity for Magic, the Elves rely solely upon it to make their lives easier. This over-reliance on magic was the main reason their forces were defeated when faced with Humanity's superior might. Alalsasaria is what you'd normally consider a "Magical Kingdom" in generic fantasy.

Their population consists of Elves for the most part, with a significant Dwarven population that lives underground and a gnomish minority that keeps to itself on Turren Island to practice *artifice* as taught to them by the human newcomers.

Although not officially part of Alalsasaria, access to the greater Underdark as well as the sunken see if mostly though Al, and as such most Dwarves "come" from that region. The dwarves live in small states that skirmish over the scarce resources of the Underdark and live in Cities illuminated by a Faux sun to simulate the day/night cycle.

### Religion

The Elves have a complex religion consisting of 13 gods. Each of them was once a mortal being that wielded great power and enacted great change upon Elvenkind, and ascended to godhood after their passing. The last of these gods was Andlat, who passed away at the end of the *Separation War* 5000 years ago.

## ...Givilith
A matriarchal society that rules over the east, Givilith was born at the end of the Separation war between the Drow rebels against the Alalsasarian Empire roughly five thousand years ago. The head of the state is "The great diviner", as Givilith queens are blessed by their goddess with the ability to see into the future. They use this ability to guide the nation into the most prosperous timeline.

After the passing of the last great diviner, there has been a disastrous succession crisis in the country as the next diviner has not been found. As the diviner is generally chosen by the goddess's guidance, this has caused great chaos, and news have stopped arriving from the Capital city.

Givilith has a blooming population of **Surface Drow** and a very small minority of gnomes. 

The Drow worship the goddess Kestal, who has long since passed and "ascended" into godhood, while rejecting the Elven gods that form the original pantheon.

## ...The Separation War

5000 years ago, Alalsasaria and Givilith used to be one state. At the time, the Elven god **Andlat** was next in line for ascension after passing. Then, from the depths of the underdark appeared a woman, **Kestal** who claimed to be the true successor to the elven line of ascension. 

The two "gods" argued greatly and each gathered up a great amount of support among the Elven people. This erupted into a civil war that lasted a few centuries and devastated central Alalsasaria, the war was finally put to an end when Andlat sacrificed himself to trap Kestal in the nine hells once and for all. The mages of Alalsasaria used a great amount of magic to construct a great mountain range between the two nations, physically splitting the continent into two halves.

After the war, many of the Elves that supported Kestal were mysteriously "branded" to physically resemble Kestal, making their appearance closer to that of the Drow. Nobody knows how this has come to pass, but many amongst the elves claim it to be a divine punishment from the Elven Gods for the Givilians betrayal of their faith.
## ...The Free North
The free north is a collection of city states that broke off from Alalsasarian rule a few centuries ago. While technically not really a "nation", most outsiders refer to the city states collectively as they tend to work together in military sense. Several attempts have been made to unity the cities of the free north, but all of them have failed.

Due to the nature of Free north politics, there is no official language for the state. People here speak a mixture of Dwerian, True Elvish, and Dark Elvish. Their population is a mish-mash of the races that live upon Presia and there is no official headcount.

## ...The Shattered Lands
Currently a no mans land, the islands of the sounds are collectively referred to as the shattered lands. a third of these islands belong to the Dwerelith Commission while one of the other thirds is held by the horde, leaving the last portion as a desolate land on which is the last bastion of the old government known as *The Court*.

## ...Whitneseria
A small island that suddenly floated into the continent's borders about 300 years ago, making it technically humanity's first arrival on the continent. Whitneseria is a small nation that keeps to itself and isolates its people from the outside world. Very little is known about what happens inside of its borders.

## ...The Horde
Arriving roughly in 1928 PU, the Horde is a Stratocracy, led by their god king. They consist of a mash-up of Goblinoid and orc races that were gathered together and turned into war machines. The horde's homeland is a mystery as not many people in the horde occupied island of Terrasol, let alone outside, know much about it.

As soon as the the Horde landed on Terrasol, they began massacring the local elven population. Within 4 years, their war against the elves had resulted in most of the elven population on the island being wiped out. Centuries of Celestial Elf culture was destroyed by the bloodthirsty Horde and eventually celestial elves were pushed out of the island entirely, and their population fell to roughly 80 thousand that scattered across the rest of the continent.

The population of the Horde is a mish mash of Goblins, Orcs, Trolls, hobgoblins, and bugbears, along with a very small minority of celestial elves that were captured and assimilated.

All hordish people worship their god-king Gajzar, who unifed the goblinoid people and conquered the rest of their continent a thousand years ago. Rumor has it that Gajzar still lives and rules from the shadows.
