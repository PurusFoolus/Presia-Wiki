The faint light of the evening sun showered the fields of At'aton in an orange hue as activity in the small farming town began to subside. Chal just finished working at his mother's field and was on his way to purchase some groceries from the general store before going home to make dinner.

It's been a few months since communications were cut between At'aton and the Capital city, rumor has it that Kestal has yet to choose a new Oracle to carry her divine will, and as such the floating city has gone into lockdown. To the people of this tiny town however that is not such a bad thing, though. Without meddling from the noble caste, the people have gained a level of freedom they didn't have before. Although this freedom didn't come without cost, as bandit activity has surged since then and has disrupted many of the trade routes.

As for At'aton however, the town has continued to prosper under the protection of a band of adventurers that formed to protect it. The Silver Arrow, a group of the town's most talented members that stood up and accepted the opportunity to help defend their homeland. Under their protection, At'aton continued its smooth operations.

Chal arrives home and greets his mother and sister, then he proceeds to set up the dinner table for the family. It doesn't take him long to prepare a nice warm meal for everyone to enjoy before going to get some rest. The orange hue that bathed the town soon subsides as the full moon rises into the sky as its younger brother's crescent hovers above it like a silver crown. Movement ceases in At'aton and the town slips into quiet rest.

And then there was light. A powerful ray of fiery red engulfed the night sky, overpowering the two moons and shining like a new sun in the night sky. Windows opened as the people of At'aton looked up to gaze at this phenomenon but it wasn't long until they realized that the small sun was growing larger by the minute.

Kass clashes her blade against the unknown soldier near the edge of the forest. Her whole body is sent flying backwards by an enemy mage hiding in the fooliage. This threat was like nothing she'd ever faced before. They were organized in a manner she'd never seen in the bandits and cutthroats she needed to face to protect her home. The soldier marched forward and thrust the blade of his sword directly into her chest.

The remaining members of the Silver Arrow were sent scrambling back to the At'aton, the overwhelming force of their enemy broke any semblance of order they had. The growing ball of fire approaching the town wasn't helping either. Kass, their fighter, was dead and so was their mage, Phaithon. At this point, their rogue had to abandon the rest of the team and rush towards the town to try and warn the denizens. Now only the Ranger and Warlock were left behind to try and hold the line long enough for their friend to reach home.

It was all for naught, though. As more and more soldiers began to pour out of the edge of the woods it was clear that this wasn't a simple bandit raid or even a push by an adversarial noble family, the banner that flew above the soldiers made that very clear. The soldiers marched forward without a single break in their ranks, and the last members of the Arrow broke under the onslaught. 

Before Arma the rogue could even reach At'aton, the flaming sun that had manifested above the town had descended upon the town, and its heat radiated even to the far outskirts of At'aton, there was no point going home. Arma, grasped by fear, decided to run away from home.

				-----------------------------------

Chal stared outside of his window, watching as this ball of flames engulfed the night sky. He rushed in a vain attempt to get his family to safety, but he was too late. As the faux sun touched the highest building in At'aton, it exploded with a power unforeseen by any of the townspeople, erasing the town center in an instant.

Chal lived near the outskirts of town, however, and thus his home was spared the brunt of the explosion although he wasn't unharmed. His house couldn't withstand the shockwave of the explosion, and a great portion of it collapsed immediately burying him in the rubble.

After the faux sun exploded, the soldiers that had gathered outside town marched in, slaughtering any that stood against them. The townspeople of At'aton were faced with an unstoppable force, and many perished under it. As for Chal, he was able to struggle his way out of the burning rubble but unfortunately the same could not be said for his family members. His grief froze him in place as he watched the town he once cherished fall into pieces.
