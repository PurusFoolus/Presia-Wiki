# Character Creation

1- For your stats, you roll **two sets** and you can choose between them. In case your rolls are particularly bad I'll allow you to reroll once more. 

1.5- If you'd rather use point buy instead of rolling, then your points are **increased to 31** rather than 28. 

2- Everyone starts with a free feat at level 1. This replaces any feats granted from the new age backgrounds, and is not restricted. Pick whatever you like (as long as it's official material). Additionally, **Variant Human is Banned** 

3- If you decide to roll for HP rather than pick average, then you can **reroll 1s**. 

4- You gain a number of points equal to your Intelligence **modifier**. You can use these to gain a language/tool proficiency (1 point cost), Skill proficiency (2 point cost), or a skill expertise (3 point cost). 

5- Homebrew is fine but I'd like to give it a read first (will probably allow it). Any content in the *Player Options* section of the main page is automatically allowed ofc.
# General Play

1- I am going to be using the system shock rules. If you take more than half your HP in one turn, you must make a **DC 15** con save or be debuffed in one way or another. This varies from "can't take reactions" to "you drop to 0 HP". 

2- Healing potions give the maximum possible HP when used as an action, and you have to roll if you use the potion as a bonus action. Giving an ally a potion uses your action and you have to roll. 

3- You can spend your action and bonus action to gain an increased movement speed of 5x your base speed, however you must only move in a straight line. 

4- If you attempt an ability check, you cannot help someone with the same ability check unless you have expertise in that skill
