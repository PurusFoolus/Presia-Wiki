Dragonborn are a magical Hybrid race combining the sturdiness and power of a great Dragon and the craftiness of a Human. A race distinct by their colorful scales and their powerful draconic breath. However, Dragonborn distinctly lack a Dragon's wings, robbing them of their ability to fly. Dragonborn are fiercely loyal to their homeland and those they consider close.

# Nature of Dragonborn
Being an artificial race, the Dragonborn possess unique qualities that differentiate them from the more common "natural" races, and it's reflected in their culture and biology.

## Reproduction for the Dragonborn

When the dragonborn were created. They were created to be a sterile race to be produced on demand and those that outlived their usefulness would be released to eventually die. However, under unknown circumstances, the waters flowing from the *Ruhig* mountain were  injected with a magical solution that cured their sterility. <br>
Theories on how this happened vary, but since then the dragonborn have gained the ability to reproduce through laying eggs. The mother lays 2-4 eggs per pregnancy and the eggs take 8 – 12 weeks of incubation before they hatch into a dragonborn hatchling, which is about as large as a human infant. Dragonborn females are only fertile once every 10 years, which makes the number of dragonborn quite low in relation to humans.

## Dragonborn Culture

Dragonborn are a hardy race and prefer to live in harsh conditions. Their society is focused around the *Ruhig* mountain range in Dwere and the *Thorakk* region in Presia, which is considered one of the harshest deserts on the continent and was named after the first *Great Wyrm* whose blood was used in their creation. <br>
Dragonborn usually prefer light clothing to reflect the environment they live in, though heavy metallic armor is their choice for defense. Dragonborn prefer darker gems for decoration and accessories, usually onyx or Black Jade and it's considered essential for a dragonborn to receive one for their wedding.  <br>
The dragonborn were created to help those in need and further the wishes of the first Emperor and as such most of them are lawful and tend to be good, opting to help those in need even to their own detriment. Being created to be war machines, most dragonborn would rather live a life of adventure and battle than stay behind to focus on more scholarly tasks and a majority of them end up working as mercenaries or adventurers. However not all of them are motivated in this fashion and some stay behind to help their community and care for their young, which unfortunately leads to most dragonborn never knowing their parents well or at all.

## Dragonborn Religion
The Dragonborn worship the [[R - The Human Pantheon (Rewrite)|human gods]], as mandated by the republic. Traditionally dragonborn are more prone to worshipping Mors, Bellum, and Vita as the gods represent the beliefs that most dragonborn hold dear to their hearts.

# History of the Dragonborn
Dragonborn have had a rich history from their creation to their current standing in the Presian and Dwerian continents.

## A Draconic Birth

Dragonborn were created from the blood of dragons, the court wizard of king Moringth I (Later known as the Erse Kaiser), who ascended to godhood to become the deity Bellum, was tasked with creating a race that would rival the strongest of giants. The court wizard deduced that the only thing capable of rivaling the giants is the dragons, and as such, his work began.  <br>
He recruited many adventurers, one of which was Lady Selia who was tasked to slay the *Great Wyrm* Thorrak. She delivered his divine blood to the court wizard. As a reward for her bravery and strength, she was awarded with becoming the first to be reborn as a dragon.

## The War of Dwere

Soon after the dragonborn were created, king Moringth employed the help of his new super soldiers in war against the other kingdoms and states in the old continent, and he was able to crush the resistance and entirely conquer his homeland. After the war, he had nothing to do with them, so he awarded them a piece of land near their birthplace for them to live the rest of their lives, expecting the race to die out soon after.

## Years of "Peace"

After the war ended and the dragonborn were given their own land to govern, many dragonborn wandered the lands working as mercenaries or adventurers, their sterility made them unable to settle down and they found themselves lacking a purpose. Although their sterility was eventually cured, dragonborn continued to roam the land of the newly unified *Dwere* seeking a new purpose. <br>
In the centuries following the war, the dragonborn were often employed by the Dwerian peace force, a psuedo-military that was created by the Empire to enforce its authority over the farther regions of the Empire. The Dragonborn came to be known as a symbol of the republic's iron fist and tyranny.

## The Civil War

Tension between the regions of the empire started to rise at the end of the 18th century eventually broke out in the city of [Placeholder] and the Empire fell into civil war. <br>
The war lasted from 1771 to 1782 and was disastrous for many regions of the empire, and causing the Great [Capital City] fire of 1781. Throughout the war, Dragonborn mercenaries were employed by both sides of the war. Nearing the end, Lord Edmund of the Imperial Army sided with the [disgruntled states name] and assisted in forcing a coup and deposing the last Kaiser, Tiana VI. <br>
Following the end of the war, the nation was restructured into a constitutional Republic, and the Dwerian Parliament was established, and the Dwerian peace force was abolished. <br>
Many years of Peace followed after the Dwerian Republic came into being, and huge advancements were made in the way of science which gave birth to Artifice, and the [[L - Warforged (WIP)| Warforged]] being created led to the Dragonborn losing many of their positions in the Dwerian military/police.

## The Expedition to Presia

In the year 1946 when the first Dwerian scout ships had discovered the existence of Presia, the *Ruhigians* were one of the first to join the expedition, sending many of their people to help scout out this new land. Dragonborn were once again enjoying the life they were created to lead. <br>
The First Contact war required a great amount of manpower, much more than the Warforged could provide. A wa took years to finish, and concluded in the loss of the exterior island of Noarun and a massive loss for the forces of the elves. Dragonborn were then granted the region of Thorakk on the shattered lands.

## Dragonborn on Presia

Unlike the Warforged, who are mostly comprised of unthinking machines, Dragonborn are not popular on the Presian frontier. The Elven Empire held dragons Sacred, and Dragonborn are a living mockery of the great Wyrms that established the Alalsasarian Empire. As such, Dragonborn are very often barred entry to the Empire.
