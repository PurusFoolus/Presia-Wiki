# Description

The Divine Element is the accumulation of the Divine Power of the ancient gods. Currently, it's been placed under Mekane's protection. The element itself has no shape, and if observed simply manifests as the viewer's ideal image of power. 

# Creation

During the Seeker wars, the ancient gods fell and their powers were spread among the others. Near the end of the war, the seekers became incredibly worried that without anyone to regulate the power of the gods, the incredible chaos will simply cause the world to collapse. Thus, they employed the assistance of **Mekane** in order to keep it safe.

The seekers employed the assistance of many extraplanar entities during their crusade, and after the dust settled they decided to grant access of the Element to several of them in order to help regulate the world. They formed [[I - The Council of Overseers]] and assigned a Seeker to be the fifth member.
