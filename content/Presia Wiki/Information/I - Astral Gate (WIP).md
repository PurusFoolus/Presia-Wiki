# Description

Astral gates are a device constructed by Srebruss during the Interplanar War against the [[I - The Council of Overseers|Overseers]]. They allow for interplanar travel between the material plane and a pre-programmed outer plane. The Gates are large circular frames, engraved with runes that channel power directly from the [[I - The Divine Element (WIP)]], allowing for creatures and objects passing through to be transported to wherever the operator designates.

# Known Locations

## Severed Eye
An Astral gate exists beneath Allahil under the control of Euris's Severed Eye, and is currently being employed by Ramiel to achieve her goal

## Broken Gate

Beneath the Chatire region, a replica astral gate that was constructed by the Drow of Vlyn'darastyl. Instead of access to the **Divine Element**, the replica gate was powered by the Elven god **Andlat**.
