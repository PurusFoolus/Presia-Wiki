Hello traveler, welcome to the world's most vibrant continent. You've arrived at the perfect time for adventure and intrigue! Please, let me introduce you to the wonders of this ancient land.

![[World Map.png]]

Presia is one of three known continents that are spread across this globe and is roughly the size of real world Europe; It's also the Elvish homeland (among other races). Due to recent events, the outside world has crept into Presia and have made parts of it their home.

(For a quick way to navigate this wiki, please check out the [[Glossary]] page)

# Location

The Presian continent is Vast and formed out of 6 major nations, [[N - Quick Overview|you can find a brief overview of them here.]]<br>
These nations are The [[N - Dwerelith (Rewrite)|Colony of Dwerelith]], [[N - Alalsasaria (WIP)|Alalsasarian Empire]], [[N - Givilith (WIP)|The Kingdom of Givilith]], [[N - Horde Occupied Terrasol (WIP)|The Hordish Occupied Territories]], [[N - The Free North (WIP)|The Federation of the Free North]], and [[N - Whitneseria (rewrite)|Whitneseria]].
<br>
Most of my games take place in Dwerelith and Alalsasaria for the time being, so these two locations are the ones that are more important to read IMO and will get their pages first.

# Races

Unlike most generic fantasy settings, the most populous lineage on this continent is the [[L - Elves (Rewrite)|Elves of Alalsasaria]], followed by the Humans of Presia. The Geographical spread  is as follows in descending order of population:

1- Dwerelith: Humans -> Elves -> Halflings -> Dragonborn -> Warforged.<br>
2- Alalsasaria: Elves -> Gnomes -> Dwarves -> Celestial Elves.<br>
3- Givilith: Drow -> Gnomes -> Dwarves.<br>
4- The Horde: Goblins -> Hobgoblins -> Orcs -> Bugbears.<br>
5- Whitneseria: Humans -> Yuan-ti.<br>
6- The Free North: Elves = Humans -> a mixture of the remaining races.

## Replacements

**[The following options *replace* traditionally available options via first party material, and will be used in its stead]** <br>
[Presian Human (by yours truly)](https://homebrewery.naturalcrit.com/share/e6ivF4OBuqmh) - Replaces Standard Human. <br>
*Humans are a deeply religious species, and their gods are ever-present in their lives. Be it Vis descending to bless a farmer's lands herself or Bellum standing on the front-lines of humanity's armies, they've made their presence well known, and their blessings upon humans are clear to all.* <br>
**[I typically give a free feat at level one, and so Variant Human isn't allowed, and this one fits the lore of the Dwerian humans in my setting a bit more.]** <br>
**Whitneserian Human** - Replaces Goliath <br>
*The tribes of Whitneseria originated from the great Ru'us mountain range, a harsh environment that conditioned them to withstand unbelievable levels of cold, this resilience was further amplified with Thabeen magic in order to allow the Human slaves to endure the hard work they were forced to do.* <br>
**[I don't really have a place to put the Goliaths in geographically, and the Whitneserian humans needed something to differentiate them from the Dwerian humans who are fundamentally different, so the Whitneserian humans gain the stats of the Goliath race while actual "Goliaths" don't exist.]** <br>
**Deep Folk** - Replaces Duergar<br>
*Deep underneath the continent of Presia lie the cities that occupy the western shores of the Unterzee. Following the ever increasing threat of monsters and hellish crawlers they created the Deep Folk to act as their first line of defense against the horrors that Lurk in the Underdark. Each deep folk has their own funeral prepared before their departure.*<br>
**[Nothing against the Duergar but I never was the biggest fan of their lore, and I like the idea of super soldiers who are dead to society and yet make sure everyone sleeps at night. Also they have seers that overdose on shrooms to see the future so they're pretty cool I'd say.]**

## Modified Content

**[This is Content that I've chosen to modify slightly to fit my world lore, but isn't outright replaced or restricted. Just a little change here or there]**<br>
**Elves (Drow, Surface)** - Loses sunlight sensitivity<br>
*The Cities of the Drow are constantly shielded from the sun, yet their bodies have evolved to acclimate to the constant bombardment of solar radiation. The Drow of the surface have long ceased to be aversely affected by the sun's warm embrace.*<br>
**Elves (Drow, Abyssonian)** - Gains the following feature: [Dim light doesn't impose disadvantage on your Wisdom (Perception) checks relying on sight.]<br>
*The Abyssonian drow have spent centuries adapting their eyes to the oppressive darkness of the Underdark leading their eyes to develop traits that helped them survive in the harsh environment.*<br>
**[A little buff to the Abyssonianns to make them a viable choice compared to the surface Drow.]**<br>
**Elves (High)** - Gains the following feature: [Consume a spell slot of first level or higher to sustain yourself in place of requiring food/water. You may also forego using your subrace cantrip for the day to gain the same effect]<br>
*Foregoing a connection to nature, the High Elves maintained a fraction of the magical energy that formed the Precursor Elves that once roamed our world. This allowed them to harness the power of magic around them to sustain their physical form.*

*Bladesinging is an ancient art that is kept secret amongst High Elven society, and is rarely spoken of outside of said circles. Said to be the way of heroes, the Elves rarely teach this art to outsiders but it's not unheard of for non-elf bladesingers* <br>

## No Restrictions

[The Witch (Credit to u/king-starman)](https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-MC55eRfjG8uBEl-qHOa) - No Restrictions. <br>
*Witchcraft is an art that flourished within Presia for thousands of years, using the powers of nature to manipulate the world around them and cursing those that stood in their search for forbidden or obscure knowledge. The practice of witchcraft was concentrated in the island of Noarun where the Witch Covens amassed a great following. However witches existed everywhere else on the continent too, though in less organized groups. After The First Contact War, the covens of Noarun were mostly destroyed, and witchcraft outlawed within the Human Colony.* <br>
[Firearms (Credit to u/IonutRO)](https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-LaQ-9bfLBNNBm-hoMee) - Firearm Proficiency <br>
*Gunpowder was essential for the Dwerian conquest of Noarun. The elven sword and spell were no match for the explosive firepower of guns and cannons. Castle walls fell, and Covens burned as humanity advanced through their lands. Guns played a crucial role in stopping the Hordish advance.* <br>

[Awakened Undead](https://walrock-homebrew.blogspot.com/2018/12/race-awakened-undead-fifth-draft.html) - No Restrictions<br>
*After the Eruption of Mount Fjall in 5260, undead all across Presia burst through the ground and wandered the earth once more. Panic spread through the cities as they were swarmed by armies of Zombies and Skeletons. Throughout the mess, though, a new group of undead arose. Those who returned to life but retained their memories from the days they were alive alongside their sentience. They roam the land with purpose and a mysterious light behind their eyes.*
