Welcome to Presia! The world's most eventful continent. You've arrived at the perfect time for adventure and intrigue! Please, let me introduce you to the wonders of this land.


![[World Map.png]]

Presia is one of three known continent that are spread across this globe and is roughly the size of real world Europe; It's also the Elvish homeland (among other races). Due to recent events, the outside world has crept into Presia and have made parts of it their home.

# Player Options

This section is dedicated to homebrew I officially endorse and also replacements/restrictions placed on already WOTC options.

[As Is the nature with most Homebrew, their spell lists rarely ever get updated post-release, as such many of these will be missing spells that were released in official material later. If you wish to include one of those, make sure it is thematically appropriate for the class and if so feel free to include it, but please give me a heads-up.]

Don't forget to follow the Character Creation rules in [[Table Rules]].

[Presian Human (by yours truly)](https://homebrewery.naturalcrit.com/share/e6ivF4OBuqmh) - Replaces Standard Human.
*Humans are a deeply religious species, and their gods are ever-present in their lives. Be it Vis descending to bless a farmer's lands herself or Bellum standing on the frontlines of humanity's armies, they've made their presence well known, and their blessings upon humans are clear to all.*
[I typically give a free feat at level one, and so Variant Human isn't allowed, and this one fits the lore of humans in my setting a bit more.]

[The Witch (Credit to u/king-starman)](https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-MC55eRfjG8uBEl-qHOa) - No Restrictions.
*Witchcraft is an art that flourished within Presia for thousands of years, using the powers of nature to manipulate the world around them and cursing those that stood in their search for forbidden or obscure knowledge. The practice of witchcraft was concentrated in the island of Noarun where the Witch Covens amassed a great following. However witches existed everywhere else on the continent too, though in less organized groups. After The First Contact War, the covens of Noarun were mostly destroyed, and witchraft outlawed within the Human Colony.*

[The Seeker (Credit to u/FriskyRisque)](https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-NM4yxEsw7dQbdWYpCRA) - Restriction: Drow Only.
*Oracles are a special brand of Clerics that are given direct power and communications with the Drow goddess Kestal. The goddess handpicks every single one, and grants them divine visions of the future to help guide their path for the betterment of Drowkind. Not all Oracles are granted the same authority, though, and the goddess definitely has her favorites.*

[The Inventor (Credit to u/KibblesTasty)](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5e7eab9fcc76e2321541f8b3/t/62d708fb6a4bcc5aaf59af5d/1658259716353/Inventor2.2.3.pdf) - Restriction: Human Adjacent
*Artifice is an entirely human-made concept, the rationalization of the natural magics that penetrate this land into an organized science. With it, humans have been able to mix Advanced technology with magic to propel their civilization into greater heights than ever achieved before.*
[This doesn't *replace* The artificer class, but it's a "sidegrade." Feel free to use this instead of Artificer (but you can't multiclass between them). Heads up that this class is *huge* so it might take a minute to navigate it]

Wizard: Bladesinging - Restriction: Elf Adjacent
*Bladesinging is an ancient art that is kept secret amongst High Elven society, and is rarely spoken of outside of said circles. A perfect mixture of dancing, spellcasting, and swordplay; It's the greatest tool for a spellblade out on the battlefield to dominate their enemies and keep themselves safe. Said to be the way of heroes, the Elves rarely teach this art to outsiders but it's not unheard of for non-elf bladesingers*
[I'm not outright banning it for non-elves but you'll have to give me a reason why an elf would teach you this sacred art]

[Firearms (Credit to u/IonutRO)](https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-LaQ-9bfLBNNBm-hoMee) - No Restriction
*Gunpowder was essential for the Dwerian conquest of Noarun. The elven sword and spell were no match for the explosive firepower of guns and cannons. Castle walls fell, and Covens burned as humanity advanced through their lands. Guns played a crucial role in stopping the Hordish advance.*
[This provides additional options and features to flesh out the barebones rules provided in the base game]
