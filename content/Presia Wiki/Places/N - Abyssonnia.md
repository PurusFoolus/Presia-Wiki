# Overvieew

Abyssonnia was a kingdom in the Central Underdark that was formed by the *Underdark Drow* in an attempt to push off the retreating Dwarves following their defeat at the hands of the Alalsasarian Elves roughly ~15000 years ago. The city states of the Drow united together and were locked in conflict with the Dwarves that formed [[N - Aetheria|Aetheria]] until [[H - The Great Flood|The Great Flood]] destroyed it.

# History
Due to the flood, not much is known of ancient Abyssonnia due to the destruction of most of their records. However, many retellings survive in Aetherian Records. Much of this information is called into questioning due to the bias the dwarves may hold, but modern day Abyssonnians are cagey about their information.<br>
<br>It is believed that the kingdom was established properly in ~10000 BS, when the Alalsasarian Elves pushed the Dwarves underground at the end of the *Elven Dark Age*. The Abyssonnians banded together in an attempt to defeat their homeland from the fleeing dwarves. Due to this, most of the Dwarves (Later becoming known as Aetherians) settled near the western and eastern side of the Presian Underdark.<br>
<br>Little is known topside about the conflict between the Abyssonnians and the Aetherians, except that skirmishes between the two groups lasted until The Great Flood destroyed most of Abyssonnia and sank the entirety of the Central Underdark beneath a vast underground sea.

# Modern Day
Despite most of the Abyssonian cities being destroyed by the flood, the surviving cities and communities still refer to themselves as Abyssonian even though they lack any form of central government. Rumor on thee surface circulate that the Abyssonnians still maintain communications and thus a form of organized government, though such rumors are unconfirmed.<br><br>

There are very few cities that have survived The Great Flood, and even many of those ended up stranded in the middle of a wide sea completely cut off from civilization and are presumed lost. The most notable exception is **Yuethindrynn**, which now serves as the remnant of the original Abyssonnian government.
