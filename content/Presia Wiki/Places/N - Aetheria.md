# Overview
Aetheria is not a nation, rather it's a unique identity embraced by the Dwarves that live beneath the surface of Presia. Named so due to their cities having a Fake sky to simulate the day/night cycle of the surface. Aetheria consists of many city-states that lie close to the coast of the Unterzee that was formed by [[H - The Great Flood|The Great Flood]] and generally act as the first line of defense between the horrors of the underdark and the people of the surface.

# History

Following their defeat at the hands of the newly established Alalsasarian Empire (Around 10000 BS), the dwarves of the surface elected to flee underground to escape the ire of the elves. *The Elven Dark Age* had reached an end and the Dwarves found themselves unwelcome upon the lands they once ruled.<br><br>
Refusing to accept the loss of their status and being reduced to "equals" of the elves, the Dwarven government elected to use their magic to "bury" their cities underground, transporting them into the Underdark.<br><br>
This caused quite the ruckus in the underdark, and led the Abyssonian elves to band together forming the [[N - Abyssonnia|Kingdom of Abyssonnia]] which kept the dwarves from being able to settle in the central underdark. For the following centuries the Dwarves continued to skirmish with the surface Elves and attempted to expand further into the underdark.<br>
# Modern Day
With the Abyssonnians gone from the scene and the entirety of the central underdark sunk beneath the sea, the Aetherian cities had to withstand a multitude of fleeing underdark creatures that are escaping the Unterzee.<br><br>
The cities on the Eastern shores banded together to form **The Coalition of Blue Skies** and banded together their militaries to keep monsters (and fleeing Abyssonnians) at bay. <br><br>

However, on the western coast, the cities formed **The Order of the Deep Folk**, super soldiers who maintain order within the territories of the Western Cities. The deep folk operate separately from the cities, but are under the oversight and jurisdiction of the cities.
