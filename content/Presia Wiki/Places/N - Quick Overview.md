# Dwerelith

The sole colony of its mother nation, *Dwere*. Originally belonging to the Elves of Alalsasaria, the island of Noarun has been occupied by Dwerian forces for the past half century. Humans made first contact with the Elves in 1931 PU (Post Union), and crushed the Elven military in the *First Contact* war. Dwerelith was officially signed into existence in 1933 PU as part of a peace treaty between Dwere and Alalsasaria. 

The capital city of Dwerelith is AussenStadt, which lies on the eastern coast of the island. The nation is governed by the *Dwerelith Commission*, under direct supervision of the Dwerian Parliment. 

Humans have low natural affinity for magic, and through many decades of trial and error, the art of *Artifice* has been born. Artificers form the upper caste of Human society. Thanks to them, Humanity's military might has advanced massively, with the invention of *Firearms*, *primitive land and air vehicles*, and ***The Warforged*****

### Species Composition

**Humans**: Due to great colonization efforts Humans are now the dominant species on the island of Noarun. Most cities are settled by and governed by humans brought from abroad, with a blooming population of Humans born in Noarun.

**Elves**: Many of the indigenous natives that used to live here have clung to their homes, though many have been forcefully deported into the Alalsasarian mainland. Many of the natives retain some connection to the Noan Witch Covens long after the witches were wiped out.

**Dragonborn**: Originally created as super-soldiers to serve in the *Unification War*, Dragonborn are now common folk that live amongst the humans, although much fewer in number.

# [[N - Alalsasaria (WIP)|Alalsasaria]]

The ancient empire of Alalsasaria used to span over the entire continent at one point in time, but now they are a shadow of their former selves. Following the assassination of Emperor Imhariel, his second in command (Magus Fyrita) has now taken the throne. 

Due to their higher affinity for Magic, the Elves rely solely upon it to make their lives easier. This over-reliance on magic was the main reason their forces were defeated when faced with Humanity's superior might. Alalsasaria is what you'd normally consider a "Magical Kingdom" in generic fantasy.

Their population consists of Elves for the most part, with a significant Dwarven population that lives underground and a gnomish minority that keeps to itself on Turren Island to practice *artifice* as taught to them by the human newcomers.

Although not officially part of Alalsasaria, access to the greater Underdark as well as the sunken see if mostly though Al, and as such most Dwarves "come" from that region. The dwarves live in small states that skirmish over the scarce resources of the Underdark and live in Cities illuminated by a Faux sun to simulate the day/night cycle.

# Givilith

A matriarchal society that rules over the east, Givilith was born at the end of the Separation war between the Drow rebels against the Alalsasarian Empire roughly five thousand years ago. The head of the state is "The great diviner", as Givilith queens are blessed by their goddess with the ability to see into the future. They use this ability to guide the nation into the most prosperous timeline.

After the passing of the last great diviner, there has been a disastrous succession crisis in the country as the next diviner has not been found. As the diviner is generally chosen by the goddess's guidance, this has caused great chaos, and news have stopped arriving from the Capital city.

Givilith has a blooming population of **Surface Drow** and a very small minority of gnomes. 

The Drow worship the goddess Kestal, who has long since passed and "ascended" into godhood, while rejecting the Elven gods that form the original pantheon.

# The Free North

The free north is a collection of city states that broke off from Alalsasarian rule a few centuries ago. While technically not really a "nation", most outsiders refer to the city states collectively as they tend to work together in military sense. Several attempts have been made to unity the cities of the free north, but all of them have failed.

Due to the nature of Free north politics, there is no official language for the state. People here speak a mixture of Dwerian, True Elvish, and Dark Elvish. Their population is a mish-mash of the races that live upon Presia and there is no official headcount.

# The Shattered Lands

Currently a no mans land, the islands of the sounds are collectively referred to as the shattered lands. a third of these islands belong to the Dwerelith Commission while one of the other thirds is held by the horde, leaving the last portion as a desolate land on which is the last bastion of the old government known as *The Court*.

# Whitneseria

A small island that suddenly floated into the continent's borders about 300 years ago, making it technically humanity's first arrival on the continent. Whitneseria is a small nation that keeps to itself and isolates its people from the outside world. Very little is known about what happens inside of its borders.

# The Horde

Arriving roughly in 1928 PU, the Horde is a Stratocracy, led by their god king. They consist of a mash-up of Goblinoid and orc races that were gathered together and turned into war machines. The horde's homeland is a mystery as not many people in the horde occupied island of Terrasol, let alone outside, know much about it.

As soon as the the Horde landed on Terrasol, they began massacring the local elven population. Within 4 years, their war against the elves had resulted in most of the elven population on the island being wiped out. Centuries of Celestial Elf culture was destroyed by the bloodthirsty Horde and eventually celestial elves were pushed out of the island entirely, and their population fell to roughly 80 thousand that scattered across the rest of the continent.

The population of the Horde is a mish mash of Goblins, Orcs, Trolls, hobgoblins, and bugbears, along with a very small minority of celestial elves that were captured and assimilated.

All hordish people worship their god-king Gajzar, who unifed the goblinoid people and conquered the rest of their continent a thousand years ago. Rumor has it that Gajzar still lives and rules from the shadows.
